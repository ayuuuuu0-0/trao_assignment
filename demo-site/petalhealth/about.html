Healthcare Compliance and Privacy First

PetalHealth was established in 2021 to bring modern cloud software to private medical practices. Our HIPAA-compliant platform is trusted by over 400 clinics.

All patient medical records are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3. We do not maintain a public job portal at this time; all staffing is conducted via private clinical recruitment partners.
