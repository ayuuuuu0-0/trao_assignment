Our Interview Process

We respect your time and want you to succeed. Our technical hiring process has four distinct stages:

  1. Initial Recruiter Screen (30 minutes): A casual conversation covering your background, career interests, and compensation expectations.
  2. Technical Coding Assessment (60 minutes): Live hands-on coding session in TypeScript or Node.js focusing on algorithmic problem solving, clean API design, and asynchronous patterns.
  3. System Architecture & Design (60 minutes): Deep dive into distributed systems, database scaling with PostgreSQL, caching strategies, and fault tolerance.
  4. Values & Cross-Functional Fit (45 minutes): Discussion with an engineering manager and product lead evaluating communication, collaboration, and mentorship.
