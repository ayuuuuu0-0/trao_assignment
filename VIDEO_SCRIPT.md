# PrepMe — 3 to 4 Minute Walkthrough Video Script

Follow this rehearsed script when recording your 3–4 minute walkthrough video for the Trao assessment submission.

### Preparation Before Recording
1. Start MongoDB, backend server (`npm run dev:server`), and web frontend (`npm run dev:web`).
2. Have `http://localhost:3000` open in your browser in full screen.
3. Have a sample job description and company URL copied to your clipboard (or use one from `test/fixtures/cases.json`).

---

### Step-by-Step Video Timeline (3:30 - 4:00 Total)

| Time Stamp | Screen Action | What to Say (Script) |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 - 0:20**<br>(Intro) | Show the **PrepMe landing page** (`http://localhost:3000`) and log in. | *"Hi! This is PrepMe, an automated, grounded interview preparation platform. Most AI prep tools hallucinate generic interview questions that miss the company's real hiring criteria. PrepMe solves this by crawling company intelligence, grounding requirements in verbatim quotes, verifying 100% must-have coverage, and compiling a deterministic study schedule with active-recall practice."* |
| **0:20 - 1:10**<br>(Kit Generation) | Click **"New Prep Kit"**. Paste the Job Description, enter `http://localhost:8099/nexusai/` (or any live URL), and click **"Generate Prep Kit"**. Show the live generation screen. | *"Here on the dashboard, I'll create a kit for a Senior Backend Engineer. When I click Generate, the server creates a background job. Notice the progress steps: our SSRF-safe crawler discovers pages while respecting robots.txt, filters relevant links like About and Careers, and queries Hacker News Algolia for real interview discussions. Even with dead URLs or sites without hiring pages, it gracefully continues without failing."* |
| **1:10 - 1:50**<br>(Grounded Requirements & Coverage) | Navigate to the **Requirements Tab** and then the **Questions Tab**. Highlight the verbatim quote in the evidence field. | *"Let's look at the generated kit. In the Requirements panel, every single requirement has an exact verbatim quote from the job description — nothing was hallucinated. In our pipeline, questions are generated in separate, prompt-engineered calls per category. Then, a deterministic TypeScript function checks coverage. If any must-have skill was missed, our coverage loop automatically triggers a second targeted pass to guarantee that zero must-haves ship uncovered."* |
| **1:50 - 2:30**<br>(Builder & Merge-Time Preservation) | In the **Question Bank**: edit a question outline inline, click save, move a question to System Design, reorder it using the arrow buttons. Then click **Regenerate Category** on Technical. | *"PrepMe is also a full interactive builder. I can edit questions or answers inline, reorder questions, or move them between categories. When I click 'Regenerate Category', notice what happens: our merge-time preservation rule calculates the replaceable set dynamically. Only unpinned, system-generated questions in this category are regenerated. My manual edits, user-added questions, and all other categories remain completely intact."* |
| **2:30 - 3:00**<br>(Practice Mode & Schedule) | Click **"Practice Mode"**. Show the 60s countdown timer, type in the answer box, press **Space** to reveal the answer, and press **4** to rate confidence. Show the summary metrics. | *"Next is Practice Mode. We simulate real interview pressure with a 60-second countdown timer and an active-recall written answer box. When ready, I can press Spacebar to reveal the reference outline and press keys 1 through 5 to rate my confidence. The queue uses spaced repetition — unseen cards first, lowest confidence first. The summary shows real-time coverage and remaining unpracticed must-haves."* |
| **3:00 - 3:30**<br>(Creative Features) | Show the **Undo Regeneration** button and click it to restore the previous snapshot. | *"For our creative features, we built the 60-Second Timed Simulation for realistic verbal practice, and Decision 17 Undo Snapshots. If an AI regeneration doesn't meet your expectations, clicking 'Undo Regeneration' rolls back to the exact snapshot taken prior to that regeneration, ensuring zero lost work."* |
| **3:30 - 3:50**<br>(Defended Design Decision & Wrap-Up) | Show the **Schedule Tab** with integer minutes and days. | *"The core architectural decision I defend is keeping schedule allocation and coverage verification 100% deterministic in pure TypeScript code rather than delegating them to the model. This guarantees exact day counts, mathematical coverage proofs, integer minutes, and consistent reliability regardless of LLM API variability. All 145 tests are passing. Thank you!"* |
